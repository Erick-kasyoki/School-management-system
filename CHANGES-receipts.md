# Payment receipts

After a payment is recorded (Finance → Fees Management → Quick Payment Recording) the receipt opens
automatically. Receipts can also be opened from Finance → **Receipts** (new menu item), from the
receipt numbers in the Fees screens, and from the 🧾 button on the Fee Payments list.

## What the receipt shows (matches the design you sent)
School logo/name/motto/address/phone/email/website · receipt no., date, academic year, term, generated by ·
QR code + barcode · student block (photo, admission no., name, gender, class, section, roll no., parent) ·
payment block (invoice, date, time, method, reference, network, cashier) · fee breakdown table ·
payment summary + notes · status box and totals · stamp and signature lines · payment history.

Everything is read from existing records (payment, student, parent, fee structure, discounts, invoices,
School Settings). The school name, motto, address, phone, email and logo come from School Settings; an
optional `website` can be added to the settings profile.

## Actions (right-hand panel)
Print Receipt · Download PDF (opens the print window — choose "Save as PDF") · Send Email ·
Share Receipt · Duplicate Receipt (prints a copy stamped DUPLICATE) · Request Refund · Cancel Receipt.
(Send WhatsApp and Send SMS are intentionally left out.)

* **Request Refund** and **Cancel Receipt** go through the approval system (Administrator review, then Principal
  approval). Nothing changes until approved; on approval the student's balance is restored.
* **Send Email** uses the same SMTP account as Messaging. The email is built on the server from the school's
  records (not from browser-supplied HTML).

## Payment form additions
Reference Number (hidden for Cash) and Network (Mobile Money only). Time and cashier are recorded automatically.
New receipt numbers are sequential: `RCP-<year>-000001`. Older receipts keep their existing numbers.
